Section 7

Blackjack Switch — Betting

Game Bet:

Fixed Total Bet: 10 Coins
You can bet a total of 10 coins, which is 5 coins for each pair of cards, and this amount cannot be changed. Since Blackjack Switch requires you to play two hands simultaneously, the total bet is split between your two hands.

Betting Structure:

  • Hand 1: 5 coins
  • Hand 2: 5 coins
  • Total: 10 coins per game

Super Match Side Bet:

Optional 5 Coin Side Bet
You can bet an additional 5 coins for the Super Match Side Bet. This is an optional bonus bet that can provide extra winnings.

How Super Match Works:
The Super Match Side Bet checks only the first 2 pairs of cards (your initial four cards across both hands). You win if you have:

  • Matching card ranks (pairs, three of a kind, or four of a kind)
  • Higher payouts for stronger matches

Payout Rules:
You can check the detailed payout rules in the game interface. The Super Match Side Bet offers various payout multipliers based on the strength of your matching cards. This bet is resolved before you make any playing decisions or switch cards.

Strategy Consideration:
The Super Match Side Bet is independent of your main game result. You can win the side bet even if you lose your main hands, and vice versa. Consider your coin balance when deciding whether to place this optional bet.
