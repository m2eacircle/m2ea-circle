Section 4

Blackjack Regular — Gameplay

Betting Phase:

Fixed Bet Amount
You can bet 5 coins per game, and this amount cannot be changed. This fixed betting structure simplifies the game and keeps focus on strategy.

Game Play:

1. Dealer's Area
The top area displays the dealer's cards. The dealer follows standard blackjack rules (typically standing on 17 or higher).

2. Your Coins and Bet Display
You can check your current coin balance and the amount you've bet for the current game.

3. Your Cards
Your hand is displayed in your playing area. You'll receive two cards initially and can take additional actions.

4. Action Buttons
Use the action buttons to Hit, Stand, Double Down, or Split (when applicable). This app allows splitting only once per game to avoid statistical complexity, as this app was developed for study purposes.

5. AI Players' Area
View the AI players' hands and their actions. This helps you understand different playing strategies and outcomes.

Game Result:

1. Your Bet Coins
The amount you wagered on this game (5 coins).

2. Your Game Result Coins
Your winnings or losses from the game. Winning pays 1:1 (you get your bet back plus an equal amount), while blackjack typically pays 3:2.

3. AI Players' Results
View how the AI players fared in the same game. This provides insights into different outcomes based on the same dealer hand.

Game Flow:
After placing your bet, cards are dealt, you make your playing decisions, the dealer completes their hand according to the rules, and results are displayed showing wins, losses, or pushes.
