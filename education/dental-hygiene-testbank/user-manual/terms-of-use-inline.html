Terms of Use (Inline Test)

When you access the Dental Hygiene Test Bank web app, you can see the 'Terms of Use' page, and when you acknowledge and agree to it, you can access the Dental Hygiene Test Bank web app.

You don't have to log in to the Dental Hygiene Test Bank web app, so the 'Terms of Use' must be checked on each device. For example, if you already checked the 'Terms of Use' from your laptop, but if you access it from your cell phone, you should check it again.
