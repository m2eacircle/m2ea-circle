Section 5

Pause

Pausing Your Test:

1. Pause During Test
During the test, you can pause the test at any time.

2. Pause Options
After pausing the test, you can:

  • Save the current progress — Save and exit to continue later
  • Resume test — Continue from where you paused
  • Back to topic selection screen — Exit without saving (progress will be lost)
