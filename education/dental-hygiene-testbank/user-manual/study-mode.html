Section 9

Study Mode

Using Study Mode:

1. Enable from Subject Selection
You can select the Study Mode from the subject selection screen.

2. Enable from Topic Selection
Or you can select the Study Mode from the topic selection screen.

3. No Time Limitation
On Study Mode, there is no time limitation, allowing you to learn at your own pace without pressure.

Study Mode is perfect for reviewing material thoroughly and understanding concepts without the stress of a timer.
