Flag

Flagging Questions:

1. Flag During Test
During the test, you can flag the test question for later review.

2. Flag from Pause Screen
On the pause screen, you can also flag the test question.

3. Check Flagged Questions
You can check the flagged question numbers and review them.

4. Test and Review
You can test and review the flagged questions specifically to focus on challenging items.
