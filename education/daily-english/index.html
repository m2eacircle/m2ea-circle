About Daily English

Learn English one day at a time

Daily English is designed for Korean speakers who want to improve their English naturally through real-life expressions, everyday dialogue, and practical vocabulary.

The Daily English app provides bite-sized lessons drawn from authentic English content such as TV shows, films, and everyday conversation. Each session focuses on a handful of expressions with Korean translations, so you always understand what you're learning and why it matters.

There is no complex grammar theory — just natural phrases in context. Open the app when you have a few spare minutes: on your commute, during a break, or before bed. Consistent short sessions outperform occasional long study marathons.

If you find any errors, have suggestions, or want to request specific topics, please contact m2eacircle@gmail.com. Click the icon below to access the app, and refer to the user manual for detailed guidance. Enjoy learning!
